A distance, not a probability. The commercial version of this report converts the distance into a percentage chance of default using a database of companies that actually defaulted, built over decades. That database is the part that is not free, and the textbook substitute is wrong in a specific direction — it returns 0.000000% for companies that do sometimes default. So no percentage is quoted here.
