Dip Finder

Finds strong, rising stocks that just dipped — and for each one tells you a buy price, a stop-loss (where to sell to cap your loss), a target, and how many shares to buy so one bad trade never costs more than you allow.
What is this & how do I read it? (2-minute read)

What the scan does

It goes through hundreds of large US and European companies and keeps only the ones that are in a long-term uptrend but had a small recent dip toward a price level where buyers stepped in before ("support"). Buying a strong stock on a dip near support is a classic swing-trading setup: your stop-loss can sit close by, so a losing trade stays cheap, while the recent high gives a realistic target.

It then removes the risky cases automatically: loss-making companies, stocks reporting earnings in the next days (earnings can gap straight through a stop-loss), stocks that barely trade, and everything when the overall market itself is falling.

How to read a pick

  • Price — the latest price; roughly where you'd buy.
  • Stop-loss — place a sell-stop order here with your broker. If the price falls this far, the idea failed; the stop caps your loss at the shown € at risk.
  • Target — the recent high the price could realistically return to.
  • Reward:Risk — 3.0 means you stand to win €3 for every €1 you risk.
  • Shares — sized so that hitting the stop-loss loses at most your "max risk per trade" (set in the filters).
  • Score — overall quality, 0–100. 70+ strong, 50–69 okay, below 50 weak. Each pick shows a one-line reason for its score.

How to use it

  • Press Run scan. The first scan downloads a year of prices and takes a few minutes — the progress log shows what it's doing. Re-runs with tweaked filters take seconds.
  • Start with the Top picks cards, then open the full table. Hover any column header or filter name for a plain-English explanation.
  • Optional: paste your holdings and cash under Filters → Portfolio — picks then get checked against your available cash, sector concentration, and total risk.
  • "Why stocks were filtered out" at the bottom shows what the scan rejected and why — useful to see if your filters are too strict.
  • The Track record section grades past picks against what the price actually did afterwards — hit target or hit stop first — so you can see whether this method earns its keep before trusting it with money.
Prices and fundamentals come from Yahoo Finance (free, may be delayed ~15 min). This is a screening tool, not investment advice — always check a chart and the news before placing a trade.
